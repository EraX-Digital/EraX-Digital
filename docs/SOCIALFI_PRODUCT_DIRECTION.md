# EraX Social — product direction

EraX Social is the social layer of EraX Digital. The company site describes a connected ecosystem for social networking, digital ownership, wallet infrastructure, and creator-focused experiences. The product tagline remains Connect. Create. Own.

People can register with email and use posts, follows, communities, and messages without a wallet. Sign-in with Ethereum and Sign-in with Solana, and linking extra addresses, stay optional. A linked wallet is shown only when this database has a signature-verified, connected row.

Creator registration and SOL or ETH tips stay off until the programs are deployed and indexed. The profile and the wallet creator page say that directly. Indexed `chain_program_events` rows can be counted. They are not turned into earnings, prices, or a token balance. There is no EraX token in this product.

Self-declared creator status is a profile field. It is not a verification badge and it is not an on-chain registration.

Direct messages remain private to the two participants. They are not end-to-end encrypted. Likes and the activity tab are visible only to the account owner. Private profiles stay hidden from people who do not follow them. A person who has been blocked cannot open the blocker’s profile. The blocker can still open the blocked profile in order to unblock.

Reports are stored for the signed-in reporter and written to the audit log. This version does not hide the reported post, comment, profile, or community by itself, and it does not add a public moderation queue.
